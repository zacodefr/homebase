The Story Behind HomeBase

Hello! I'm Melissa Versel, a student at The Davis Academy, and I chose to create HomeBase for my tech fair project because I saw a need in my own home.

I'll admit it: I wasn't always great at listening when I had a chore to do. But then I had an idea.

I tend to put things off because, for some reason, I feel like I need to have a screen in front of me to get things done (even though I really don't!). So I thought, why not create an app that meets that need?

That's how HomeBase was born.

I also got inspiration while scrolling on my phone and coming across videos of a home device that keeps track of schedules, calendars, and more. I can't remember what it was called, but the idea stuck with me.

I mean, what kid wouldn't be attracted to a random screen sitting in the kitchen when they walk in? That was my thought!

My original goal for HomeBase was to help my own family. And I believe it could help yours, too.
